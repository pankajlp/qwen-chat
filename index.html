Skills

Skills are packaged instructions for particular kinds of work: test-driven development, debugging, research, PDFs, spreadsheets and more. Type / in any chat to use one, for example /grilling my plan to switch jobs. In Code and Accounts mode Qwen also picks the right skill by itself.

Skills live in qwen-chat/skills. To add your own, make a folder there with a SKILL.md (name and description at the top), then reload. Sources and licences: skills/SOURCES.md.

Connectors

Let Qwen search and read your email, calendar and files, and draft emails, invites and posts for you. Qwen talks to Google, Microsoft and X directly from this Mac, sign-ins are kept in your macOS Keychain, and none of this is reachable from the public link. Qwen always shows you an email, invite or post and waits for your OK before it goes out.
